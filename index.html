01 / ABOUT

Research Overview

My research focuses on understanding the complex interplay between interparticle interactions, solvent transport, evaporation kinetics and structural evolution during evaporation-induced assembly of colloidal droplets. A major objective is to elucidate how particle size, interactions and drying dynamics govern hierarchical organization and enable the encapsulation of biological and synthetic polymers within nanoparticle shells. By combining small-angle scattering experiments, electron microscopy and particle dynamics simulations, I investigate the structural pathways connecting colloidal dispersions to functional dry microgranules.

02 / RESEARCH

Research Interests

Evaporation-Induced Assembly

Investigating particle migration, concentration gradients, interparticle correlations and shell formation during solvent evaporation.

Bio-polymer Encapsulation

Understanding hierarchical silica–nanocellulose architectures and the role of particle size stratification in polymer encapsulation.

Structural Characterization

Investigating nanoscale and mesoscale organization through small-angle X-ray and neutron scattering.

Particle Dynamics Simulation

Computational modelling of particle transport, interparticle forces, aggregation and hierarchical packing during droplet drying.

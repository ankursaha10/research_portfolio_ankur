01 / ABOUT

Research Overview

My research focuses on understanding the complex interplay between interparticle interactions, solvent transport, evaporation kinetics and structural evolution during evaporation-induced assembly (EIA) of colloidal droplets. The central objective is to understand how particle size, interactions and drying dynamics govern the formation of hierarchical structures and enable the encapsulation of biological and synthetic polymers within nanoparticle shells. By combining small-angle scattering experiments, electron microscopy and particle dynamics simulations, I investigate the structural pathways connecting colloidal dispersions to functional dry microgranules.

02 / CURRENT RESEARCH

Research Interests

01. Evaporation-Induced Assembly

Understanding particle migration, concentration gradients, interparticle correlations and shell formation during solvent evaporation from colloidal droplets.

02. Bio-polymer Encapsulation

Investigating hierarchical silica–nanocellulose architectures and the role of particle size stratification in polymer encapsulation within spray-dried microgranules.

03. Structural Characterization

Exploring nanoscale and mesoscale organization using small-angle X-ray and neutron scattering techniques.

04. Particle Dynamics Simulation

Developing computational models to investigate particle transport, interparticle forces, aggregation and hierarchical packing during droplet drying.

03 / METHODOLOGY

Experimental & Computational Approaches

Integrating scattering, imaging and computational approaches to establish structure–property relationships in colloidal microgranules.
